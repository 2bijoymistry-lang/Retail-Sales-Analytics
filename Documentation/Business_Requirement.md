Retail Sales Analytics
Business Problem

A retail company wants to analyze its sales data to improve business performance.


The management wants answers to the following questions:

Which products generate the highest sales?
Which product categories are the most profitable?
Which customers contribute the highest revenue?
Which cities and states generate the most sales?
Which employees perform best?
What is the monthly sales trend?
Which products should be promoted?
Which products have low sales?
What are the overall business recommendations?


Project Objective

To analyze retail sales data using SQL, Excel, Python, and Power BI and provide actionable business insights that help management make data-driven decisions.

Tools Used:
SQL
Excel
Python
Power BI

This may seem simple, but interviewers love candidates who can explain the business problem before jumping into code.
