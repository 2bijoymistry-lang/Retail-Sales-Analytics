## 📊 Project Overview

This Retail Sales Analytics project analyzes retail transaction data to identify important sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and regional performance.

The project combines data preparation, exploratory analysis, SQL-based analysis, and Power BI visualization to transform raw retail data into actionable business insights.

The final dashboard provides an interactive view of key business metrics and allows users to explore sales performance through filters and visualizations.

## ❓ Business Questions Answered

1. What are the overall sales and revenue trends?
2. Which products generate the highest sales?
3. Which categories perform the best?
4. Which regions contribute the most to sales?
5. How does sales performance change over time?
6. Which customers or customer segments contribute significantly to revenue?
7. What products or regions require additional attention?
8. What are the key trends that can support business decision-making?

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Identified overall sales performance and major revenue trends.
- Compared product and category-level performance.
- Analyzed regional contribution to overall sales.
- Identified high-performing and low-performing products.
- Examined changes in sales performance over time.
- Used interactive Power BI filtering to explore business performance from multiple perspectives.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing products and categories to maximize revenue.
- Investigate underperforming products and regions.
- Use historical sales trends for better inventory planning.
- Identify valuable customer segments and strengthen customer retention.
- Monitor regional performance to identify growth opportunities.
- Use dashboard KPIs for regular sales-performance monitoring.
